How the Draft Land menu was made

This follows the process from the Fasan video: form an opinion first, turn the feeling into a mood board and a painting "skill", then put it together and refine it until it feels cared for.

Step 1 · The feeling

Quick, unfussy, a bit cheeky, still hand-made

quickunfussycheekyhand-madeHong Kong

Fasan's feeling is warm, slightly rustic and honest. Draft Land shares the honesty: no garnish, no long story. It adds speed and a wink. From that feeling come the visual choices: a black stamped title on Draft Land's own ivory, with aubergine text and brass from their taps, watercolour glasses with nothing on the rim, a teal "pour" stamp, a red "taster" stamp, and the Cantonese slogan 唔使急，最緊要快 ("no rush, but be quick").

Step 3 · The painting skill

Watercolour glasses, painted by hand-style AI

Like Fasan's coffee cups, the eight cocktails were painted with an image model as one watercolour sheet: loose washes, pigment blooms, white-paper highlights on the ice. Each glass was then cut out so it sits straight on the paper. Every drink is in a plain bar glass with no garnish, the way Draft Land serves them. The taster behind each drink is the same painting, served small.

Step 3½ · The lab touch

A little Le Labo

Draft Land's rooms feel like a clean lab with a row of taps, close to Le Labo's perfume labs. So the menu borrows Le Labo's label: a white typewriter sticker that says where it was freshly poured, the date, and who it is for. Type your name on the label at the top and it is printed on the label of your surprise drink. Each drink also carries a tap number, like Le Labo's SANTAL 33.

The story behind it

Why cocktails on tap?

Angus Zou (Taipei, ex-Alchemy) and Antonio Lai (Hong Kong, Quinary) had long talked about opening a bar together to mark their friendship. Their view was that cocktails had become too pricey, too scarce and too intimidating. If you batch, keg and pour them like draught beer, they become fast, consistent and cheaper, with less waste, and guests can taste before ordering. The brand's four values are speed, quality, affordability and sustainability.
